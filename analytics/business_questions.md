# Business Questions

## 1. Order & Revenue Performance

- How do order volume and revenue trend over time, and are there identifiable growth or decline periods?
- What is the average order value (AOV), and how does it vary by product category?
- Are there seasonal or calendar-based patterns in order volume and revenue?

## 2. Delivery & Logistics Performance

- What percentage of orders are delivered on time versus late?
- What is the end-to-end delivery duration from purchase approval to customer delivery?
- Which carriers or logistics paths are most strongly associated with delivery delays?

## 3. Seller Performance & Reliability

- Which sellers consistently exhibit late deliveries or order cancellations?
- How does seller performance impact delivery speed and order completion rates?
- Are high-volume sellers more operationally reliable than low-volume sellers?

## 4. Customer Experience Signals

- What proportion of orders are canceled, and at which stage of the order lifecycle do cancellations occur?
- How do delivery delays correlate with customer review scores and feedback?
- Are certain product categories more prone to cancellations, delays, or negative reviews?

## 5. Geographic & Regional Insights

- How do delivery times vary by customer geographic region?
- Are there regions that systematically experience slower deliveries or higher cancellation rates?
- How does seller location relative to the customer impact fulfillment speed?
